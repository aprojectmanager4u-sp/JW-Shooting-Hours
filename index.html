Privacy

Everything stays on this phone. There is no account, no server and no tracking. The app saves only your last location (if you set one), the species you picked and the rounding setting, in this app's storage on your phone.

Location is read only while the app is open: when you tap Use my location, and once automatically the first time you open the app. To erase the saved spot, tap Clear saved location above.

The app contacts nothing else. Fonts, the DNR tables and the county map are built in. When you open it online, the app checks GitHub Pages (where it's hosted) for updates, and GitHub sees your IP address like any web host. DNR links open in your browser only if you tap them.

Times follow Wis. Admin. Code NR 10.06: zone A times come from sunrise and sunset at Sheboygan (southern area) or Powers, MI (northern area); each zone west adds 4 minutes. Shown in Wisconsin clock time (daylight time when in effect). For 2026 the app uses the DNR's published Zone A tables directly (all four tables, every day). Other years are calculated and match published tables within 1 minute.
Always confirm with the official tables before you shoot: DNR calculating shooting hours · zone map. Property-specific closures (refuges, some wildlife areas) are not included.
