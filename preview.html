Privacy

Your data stays on this phone. There is no account, no server and no tracking. The app saves your hunting spot (if you set one), your choices and the last weather forecast, in this app's storage on your phone.

Location is read only while the app is open: when you tap Use my location, once automatically the first time you open the app, and on the Map while the locate button is on. It stops when you leave the Map or close the app. Clear saved location erases the saved spot and forecast.

Government services, only for the features you use. The National Weather Service receives your hunting spot rounded to about half a mile, to return the forecast. USGS The National Map supplies map images for the area you're viewing. With Public land on, the Wisconsin DNR's map servers draw public land for that area and look up the property at the map's crosshair. None of them needs an account or tracks you. The app also checks GitHub Pages, where it's hosted, for updates. Fonts, DNR tables and the county map are built in.

Public land boundaries are for reference. Check each property's rules, closures and posted boundaries before you hunt.

Times follow Wis. Admin. Code NR 10.06: zone A times come from sunrise and sunset at Sheboygan (southern area) or Powers, MI (northern area); each zone west adds 4 minutes. Shown in Wisconsin clock time (daylight time when in effect). For 2026 the app uses the DNR's published Zone A tables directly (all four tables, every day). Other years are calculated and match published tables within 1 minute.
Always confirm with the official tables before you shoot: DNR calculating shooting hours · zone map. Property-specific closures (refuges, some wildlife areas) are not included.
