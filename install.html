Install guide

WI Shooting Hours

Walts shooting hours for today, your location and the species you're hunting. Free, no account, works without a signal once installed.

Android

  1. Open the app link in Chrome.
  2. When it asks to use your location, tap Allow.
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right corner.
  4. Tap Install app. If you see Add to home screen instead, tap it, then Install.
  5. Tap Install to confirm.
  6. Open WI Shooting Hours from your home screen or app drawer (orange crosshair icon).

If "Use my location" doesn't work

  • Make sure Location is on: swipe down from the top of the screen and check the Location tile.
  • In Chrome, tap ⋮ → Settings → Site settings → Location. If this site is listed under Blocked, tap it and choose Allow.
  • Check that Chrome itself may use location: Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions → Location → Allow only while using the app.

To remove it

Press and hold the app icon, then tap Uninstall.

iPhone

  1. Open the app link in Safari. If you tapped it in Gmail, a text or the Google app and it opened in Chrome or Google, copy the link and paste it into the Safari app instead.
  2. When it asks to use your location, tap Allow.
  3. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom or top of the screen.
  4. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  5. Tap Add in the top right corner.
  6. Open WI Shooting Hours from your home screen (orange crosshair icon).

If you already added an icon from Chrome or the Google app, delete that icon first, then add it again from Safari. iPhones never show an "Install" button for web apps. Add to Home Screen is the install. The iPhone may ask for location again from time to time; tap Allow, or tap the map instead.

If "Use my location" doesn't work

  • Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. Make sure it's on, then tap Safari Websites and choose While Using the App.
  • Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Location (on older iPhones, Settings → Safari → Location) and choose Ask or Allow.

To remove it

Press and hold the app icon, tap Remove App, then Delete from Home Screen.

Using the app

  • Date: shows today. Tap the date to pick another day, or use the ‹ › arrows.
  • Species: tap what you're hunting. Waterfowl, early teal and spring turkey have different hours from deer.
  • Location: tap Use my location, or tap the map to set your spot. The app finds your county, Northern or Southern area, and zone A–F.
  • Next 7 days: scroll down to plan ahead.
  • Weather tab: current conditions, wind and where your scent drifts, the next 24 hours, and 7 days with wind, from the National Weather Service for your hunting spot.
  • Map tab: Topo, Air photo or Hybrid maps from USGS. Tap the locate button to see yourself as a blue dot. Tap Public land to turn Wisconsin DNR public land on or off, change its transparency, or see the legend. The panel at the bottom names the property under the crosshair, and Set as hunting spot uses that point for your hours and weather.
  • My waypoints: on the Map, tap + to save a waypoint where you stand or at the crosshair, with your choice of symbol, color, style and notes. Tap the list button to see, search and sort them, import GPX or KML files (onX, Garmin, Gaia GPS, Google Earth), and export GPX, KML or CSV. Waypoints are saved only on your phone and are never uploaded. Export a file now and then as a backup.
  • Updates: when a new version is available, an orange bar says A new version of the app is ready. Tap Update now.

The 2026 times come straight from the Wisconsin DNR's published shooting-hours tables.

Privacy: no account, no server, no tracking. Your hunting spot, settings and last forecast stay on your phone. Waypoints, tracks and areas you save or import stay only on your phone; they're never uploaded and leave the phone only when you export a file. The National Weather Service receives your spot rounded to about half a mile for the forecast; USGS and the Wisconsin DNR supply map images for the area you view. You can erase the saved location in the app with Clear saved location.

Unofficial app. Always confirm with the official Wisconsin DNR shooting hours before you hunt. Closures on specific properties (refuges, some wildlife areas) and pheasant opening-day hours are not included. Public land boundaries are for reference; check each property's rules and posted boundaries.
